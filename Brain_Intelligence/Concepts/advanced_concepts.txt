## 1. OPTIONS & DERIVATIVES

### Why Institutions Use Options:

| Strategy | Purpose | Risk |
|---------|---------|------|
| Covered Calls | Generate income | Limited upside |
| Protective Puts | Insurance | Cost |
| Bull Call Spreads | Leverage up | Defined risk |
| Iron Condors | Profit from low volatility | Defined |

### How We Use Options Thinking:

```
Even if we don't trade options:
- Understand implied volatility
- Know when options are expensive (high IV)
- Understand market expectations
- Use options to limit risk
```

## 6. MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE

### How Orders Work:

```
MARKET ORDER:    Buy/sell immediately at market price
LIMIT ORDER:     Buy/sell at specific price
STOP ORDER:      Trigger when price hits level
```

### Order Flow:

```
BUY LIMIT:     Weak hands expecting pullback
SELL LIMIT:    Taking profit
MARKET BUY:    Urgent, willing to pay up
MARKET SELL:   Panic, must get out
```

### What I Look For:

- Large market orders at support
- Buying pressure at key levels
- Absence of selling at support
- Volume spike confirming move

## 7. POSITION SIZING

### My Position Sizing Rules:

| Confidence | Position Size |
|-----------|---------------|
| Very High (90%) | 20-25% |
| High (80%) | 15-20% |
| Medium (70%) | 10-15% |
| Low (<70%) | 5-10% |

### Position Sizing Formula:

```
POSITION SIZE = (Account × Risk %) / (Entry - Stop)

Example:
$100,000 account
Risk 2% = $2,000
Entry $100, Stop $95
Position = $2,000 / ($100-$95) = 40 shares
```

## 8. TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

### Common Mistakes:

| Mistake | Why It Happens | How To Fix |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| Revenge trading | Lost money, want it back | Walk away |
| FOMO | Fear of missing out | Wait for entry |
| Overtrading | Need to "do something" | Patience |
| Averaging down | Hope stock recovers | Strict stops |
| Taking profits early | Fear losing | Let winners run |

### My Psychology Rules:

1. NEVER trade after a loss
2. Wait for setup, don't force
3. Let winners run, cut losers
4. One trade at a time
5. Review every trade weekly
