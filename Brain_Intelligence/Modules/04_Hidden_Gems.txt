# MODULE 4: HIDDEN GEMS FINDER

## FINDING THE UNDISCOVERED

Famous stocks = Already discovered = Limited upside
Hidden gems = Not discovered = HUGE upside potential

---

## OUR PHILOSOPHY

### Why Hidden Gems?

1. **Limited upside in famous stocks**
   - Already discovered
   - Price reflects value
   - Limited room to grow

2. **Hidden gems upside potential**
   - Not discovered by market
   - Price doesn't reflect value
   - Multiple expansion possible

---

## HOW WE FIND HIDDEN GEMS

### Criteria 1: Coverage Analysis

```
Low analyst coverage = Opportunity
High analyst coverage = Already discovered
```

### Criteria 2: Sector Discretion

```
Unfashionable sectors = Hidden gems
Fashionable sectors = Expensive
```

### Criteria 3: Financial Quality

```
Strong fundamentals
Undervalued price
Clear catalyst
```

### Criteria 4: Unique Position

```
Niche player
Competitive advantage
Pricing power
```
