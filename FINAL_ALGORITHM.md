# SUPER BRAIN FINAL ALGORITHM v2.0

## The 4 Principles of Winning

1. **DISCIPLINE > INTELLIGENCE**
   - Follow rules strictly
   - No emotional trading
   - Accept NO TRADE days

2. **FILTERS > PICKING**
   - Quality over quantity
   - 0 picks > bad picks
   - Wait for perfect setup

3. **TIMING > STOCK**
   - Right stock + wrong time = LOSS
   - Wrong stock + right time = LOSS
   - Right stock + right time = WIN

4. **PRESERVATION > RETURNS**
   - Protect capital first
   - Small losses OK
   - Big losses destroy game

## The 5 Secret Filters

### FILTER 1: SECTOR DIRECTION
- Check: Is sector flow positive?
- Only trade: 科技, 新能源, 医药, 消费, AI
- AVOID: 金融, 地产, 能源, 钢铁

### FILTER 2: MARKET TIMING
- Check: Is it 9:45-10:00 AM?
- NEVER buy before 9:45
- Let market stabilize first

### FILTER 3: MOMENTUM CONFIRMATION  
- Check: Is change > 0%?
- Only buy positive momentum
- Never catch falling knife

### FILTER 4: VOLUME VALIDATION
- Check: Volume > 500K?
- No volume = no institutional interest
- Volume = institutional confirmation

### FILTER 5: INSTITUTIONAL BLESSING
- Check: Smart money inflow?
- Follow institutional flows
- Institutions know more

2. Take profit at +5%

### Exit (14:30-15:00 PM)

2. Record results
